# Hero Sphere Grid and Skill Tree

**Normative References:** Decisions Q185, Q186, Q187, Q188, Q189.

---

## 1. The Sphere Grid Progression Model [Q185]

Outside combat, hero customization is driven by an interconnected **Sphere Grid** (skill tree) where players spend progression currency (Sphere Points) earned through combat victories.

### 1.1 Node Classifications [Q186]
1. **Attribute Nodes:** Permanent stat upgrades for the hero:
   - Starting Hero HP.
   - Initial Mana Capacity on Turn 1.
   - Hero Mana Cap.
   - Native Hero Armor or Shield at match start.
2. **Passive Talent Nodes:** Global combat passives:
   - Discount on the first equipment played each combat.
   - Bonus draw upon specific tribal synergies.
   - Enhanced fatigue resistance.
3. **Card Variant Upgrade Nodes [Q187]:**
   - Unlocks improved or specialized card variants (e.g. *Veteran Rampart Guard* replacing *Rampart Guard*).
   - Variant upgrades respect the 4-copy rule across the family [Q207].

---

## 2. Pathways and Archetypes [Q188]

The grid branches into distinct tactical constellations corresponding to playstyles:
- **Vanguard (Fortification):** Focuses on armor stacking, defensive lane control, and counter-attacks.
- **Mystic (Arcane Control):** Focuses on spell amplification, mana acceleration, and targeted disruption.
- **Skirmisher (Tempo & Agility):** Focuses on movement manipulation, swift strikes, and early aggression.

---

## 3. Respec and Progression Resets [Q189]

- Players may reset invested Sphere Points at designated Sanctum floors in the Tower.
- Respec refunds 100% of invested skill points, allowing full redistribution to experiment with different archetypes against upcoming boss encounters.
