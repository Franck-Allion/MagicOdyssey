## 2. The 100 Independent Cards [Q255]

The 100 independent cards are non-tribal or multi-tribal, allowing diverse deck construction and drafting flexibility:
- **50 Neutral Creatures:** Mercenary sellswords, wild beasts, clockwork golems, spirits, and ancient elementals.
- **30 Neutral Spells:** Direct burst, column teleports, line clears, debuff purges, and tactical card draws.
- **20 Neutral Equipments:** Universal weapons, heavy tower shields, enchanted rings, and runic amulets.

### 2.1 Bridge Mechanics and Cross-Tribe Synergies [Q256–Q260]
1. **Tribal Adapters:** Neutral creatures that gain bonuses when positioned adjacent to specific tribes (e.g., *Garrison Captain* granting +1 Armor to adjacent Humans or Dwarves).
2. **Universal Equipment:** Independent weapons and protections follow standard slot rules (1 Weapon, 1 Protection) and can be equipped by any tribe except Dragons (who have 0 slots) [Q253].
3. **Draft Balance:** Independent cards ensure players can always draft viable options regardless of their primary tribal alignment.
