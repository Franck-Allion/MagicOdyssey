## Reserved area for the future opponent HUD

Proposed usable envelope: **x 24..296, y 24..320**, or **272 x 296 reference pixels**. This leaves 24 reference pixels before the player panel at y=344 and a 24 px horizontal gutter to existing regions starting at x=320. At 720p the envelope is x 16..197.333, y 16..213.333, or 181.333 x 197.333 physical pixels, with a 16 px lower gutter.

Risk for R07A: 296 px of reference height cannot accommodate the player's portrait plus two vertically stacked 180 px orbs. A compact identity/portrait with a concise resource arrangement must be designed inside this budget and evaluated for 720p readability. No particular arrangement or typography is approved here. No production reservation box, enemy artwork, duplicated panel, or new opponent HUD was added. The existing opponent HUD stays at x320..680.
