# Spell frame — mandatory post-generation validation

**Protocol prepared; NOT RUN on Spell artwork because no Spell PNG exists yet.** This is an acceptance checklist for the later generated candidate, not evidence that a generation or validation has occurred.

1. Preserve the original generated candidate and record filename, provenance, image-generation prompt/reference versions, dimensions, stored color mode and SHA-256. Expected eventual source is `assets/_source/lot1/cards/mo_card_frame_spell_v01.png`; do not create an empty file. Delivery must be PNG RGBA8 at **1086×1448, exactly 3:4**. If an oversampled true-3:4 original is used, record the uniform downsample and both hashes; reject distortion, silent cropping or a mislabeled non-3:4 file.
2. Measure the **entire alpha histogram**, not just corners. Report alpha0, alpha1, alpha2–253, alpha254 and alpha255 counts. Alpha1 must be zero. Reject a full-canvas matte, broad near-opaque material interiors, checkerboard paint or an opaque illustration plug. Existing master alpha exceptions do not automatically apply to this new asset.
3. Establish explicit masks on the **actual candidate** for exterior, illustration aperture, solid bronze/enamel/panel interiors and true edge transitions. Do not infer success solely from a few seed samples or the alpha histogram. Require alpha0 on the exterior/aperture interior, alpha255 on solid material interiors, and confine intermediate alpha to real antialiased material boundaries. Measure stray components, holes and semi-transparent residue outside boundary bands. Verify dark/bright composites at 100% and source scale for colored fringes and solid-metal damage.
4. Independently measure the actual outer envelope, illustration opening, title/type/effect quiet areas, mana safe disk and three rarity substrates. Compare against [layout-reservations.json](layout-reservations.json). The illustration opening must be fully covered by the intended renderer rectangle; inspect curved corners for wedges. Exclude the three painted stones and opaque writing backings from the transparent-aperture mask. No new under-frame insert is intended; if a later approved design requires one, measure its explicit transparent cavity before acceptance.
5. Inspect RGB/material content as well as alpha. Reject baked letters/digits/pseudo-text, spell illustrations, stat symbols, empty Attack/Health rings, Armor/Shield plaques, equipment sockets, Weapon/Protection cues, or decorative panels occupying dynamic writing areas. Check no colored fringe or matte is hidden by the transparency preview. Do not silently edit the protected Creature master or globally threshold alpha. Any candidate correction must be versioned and revalidated.
6. Build **clearly labeled review-only** native-scale composition studies using the real candidate and existing fonts/content. Check hand heights 270/180 px and raised inspection heights 540/360 px at native 1080p/720p, respectively. Exercise the four normative spells, long title/effect stress samples, 0/2/3/10 mana and all five dynamic rarity treatments; inspect real rasterized text, contrast, line wrapping and material margins. No stat placeholders are allowed on Spell cards. Preserve full names and explicitly report overflow. A static study does not constitute completed Godot integration.
7. Obtain explicit **owner art approval of the real, alpha-validated Spell frame**. Record the selected file/hash and review evidence. Only then may P009-R01B be authorized. Its separate checks must cover common CardView variant integration, correct mask registration/full-frame UVs, no phantom creature statistics, original Creature rendering parity, hand picking/hover, genuine GPU captures, Windows standalone input and applicable native regressions.

The current source/implementation Epic-color mismatch is documented in the brief; keep generation neutral and resolve conformance during the relevant future review without changing existing production colors in this preparation task.
